Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Devotion.Run (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, and authentication credentials.
  • Prayer intentions and reflections: text, voice notes, and journal entries you create within the app.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support team or community features.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Health and fitness data: with your permission, we access Apple HealthKit data such as workouts, distance, calories, heart rate, and route information.
  • Location data: GPS data during active runs to track distance, pace, and route.
  • Usage data: app interactions, feature usage, crash reports, and device information (iOS version, device model, app version).
  • Third-party platform data: when you connect Strava, Garmin, or Apple Health, we receive activity data according to the permissions you grant.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Devotion.Run experience.
  • Track your runs, calculate pace and distance, and generate workout summaries.
  • Deliver daily devotionals, Bible verses, and personalized content.
  • Sync your data across devices via CloudKit.
  • Send you optional notifications, devotional reminders, and product updates.
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve performance and fix bugs.

3. Strava Integration

If you choose to connect your Strava account, Devotion.Run uses the Strava API to access and upload activity data. We only request the minimum permissions necessary:

  • Read your public profile and activity data you authorize.
  • Upload activities from Devotion.Run to your Strava account, if you enable this feature.

You can disconnect Strava at any time in the app settings. Disconnecting stops future data syncing but does not delete previously uploaded activities from Strava.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with:

  • Cloud service providers: Apple (CloudKit, HealthKit) for data storage and sync.
  • Analytics providers: to understand app usage and crashes.
  • Fitness platforms: Strava or Garmin, only when you explicitly connect those accounts.
  • Legal compliance: when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure and encrypted in transit and at rest. We implement industry-standard security measures, but no system is completely secure. We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access, update, or delete your account and personal data.
  • Revoke HealthKit, location, or Strava permissions at any time via iOS Settings.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing contato@devotion.run.

7. Children's Privacy

Devotion.Run is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including the United States, via Apple’s infrastructure. By using the Services, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us
