Features

Faith and running in
perfect harmony

Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit. Devotion.Run cares for the whole temple — body, mind, and spirit — with every workout.

📖

Daily Devotional

A unique devotional every day, connected to your workout. Read before you run and turn your run into meditation on the Word.

Purposeful Running

Set a prayer intention, run, and receive Bible verses every kilometer. Every step is an act of worship.
