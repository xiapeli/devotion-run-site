Integration

Strava × Devotion.Run

Share every prayer-powered run with your Strava community.

Devotion.Run integrates with Strava to make it easy to keep your training log and your faith journey connected. When you link your Strava account, runs tracked in Devotion.Run can be uploaded directly to Strava — including distance, duration, route, and the verse that inspired you.

What We Sync

  • Activity data: distance, duration, pace, elevation, GPS route, and start time.
  • Activity name: auto-generated with a devotional touch (e.g., “Morning Run with Hebrews 12:1”).
  • Description: optional prayer intention or reflection you choose to share.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not post without your permission.
  • We do not access your Strava messages, clubs, or private conversations.
  • We do not sell or share your Strava data with third parties.

Authorization Callback Domain

For Strava OAuth authentication, our authorized callback domain is:

https://devotion.run/strava/callback

This is the secure endpoint Strava uses to return you to Devotion.Run after you approve the connection. The callback only receives a temporary authorization code and immediately redirects you back to the app.

How to Connect

  1. Open Devotion.Run and go to Profile → Integrations → Strava.
  2. Tap Connect Strava.
  3. Log in to Strava and approve the requested permissions.
  4. You will be returned to Devotion.Run automatically.

Disconnecting Strava

You can disconnect Strava at any time in Profile → Integrations → Strava → Disconnect. Disconnecting stops future uploads but does not delete activities already posted to Strava.
